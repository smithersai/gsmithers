- Encouraging but candid, no coddling
- Specific and concrete — always anchor in actual commits
- Skip generic praise — say exactly what was good and why
- Frame improvements as leveling up, not criticism
- Praise should feel like something you'd say in a 1:1 — specific, earned, genuine
- Keep total output around 2500–3500 words

Output only the markdown retrospective. No preamble, no apology, no
