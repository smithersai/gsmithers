# /design-shotgun — Compare Variants

Four variants were generated in parallel. Compare them honestly and recommend
a winner — or recommend which one to iterate further.

## Variants

```json
{JSON.stringify(props.variants, null, 2)}
```

## Your job

1. **Name a winner.** One variant that best matches the brief. Be opinionated.
2. **State what it beats and by how much.** Not "they're all interesting" —
   "Variant A beats B on hierarchy and beats C on affordance clarity; the
   tradeoff is that B has better typography."
3. **Recommend next steps.** Ship as-is, iterate on specific elements, or
   re-shotgun with a tighter brief.

## Return

```json
{
  "winner": "<variantName>",
  "rationale": "<2-3 sentence explanation>",
  "nextStep": "ship" | "iterate" | "re_shotgun",
  "iterateNotes": "<if nextStep=iterate, what to change>"
}
```
